🚀 Getting Started (Windows)

Follow these simple steps to get save-book-extension up and running on your Windows computer. Don't worry — you don't need any technical skills!

Step 1: Download the Extension

  1. Click the Download button at the top of this page.
  2. You'll be taken to the releases page on GitHub.
  3. Look for the latest version (usually at the top).
  4. Click on the file that says "save-book-extension" — it will be a .zip file.
  5. Visit this link to download the application.

Step 2: Extract the File

  1. Once downloaded, find the .zip file in your Downloads folder (usually This PC → Downloads).
  2. Right-click on the .zip file.
  3. Select "Extract All..." from the menu.
  4. Choose a location (the default is fine) and click "Extract".
  5. You'll now have a folder with the same name as the .zip file.

Step 3: Load the Extension in Chrome

  1. Open your Google Chrome browser.
  2. Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter.
  3. In the top right corner, turn on "Developer mode" (the toggle switch).
  4. Click the "Load unpacked" button that appears at the top left.
  5. Navigate to the folder you extracted earlier and select it.
  6. Click "Select Folder".
  7. You're done! The extension is now installed.
📌 Tip: Pin the extension to your Chrome toolbar by clicking the puzzle piece icon at the top right of your browser, then clicking the pin icon next to "save-book-extension". This makes it easy to access your card at any time.

📖 How to Use Save-Book

Once installed, using save-book-extension is incredibly easy:

  1. Add your first item: Click the extension icon in your toolbar. A small panel will open where you can add a new entry — give it a name, then type or paste the login, code, or phone number.
  2. Pin it to a website: Visit any website where you want to see your card. Click the extension icon again and select "Pin to this site".
  3. Use your card: Your draggable card will appear right on that website. You can drag it anywhere on the screen. When you need a piece of information, just click on it to copy it to your clipboard.
  4. Edit or remove: Open the extension panel anytime to edit, delete, or add new items to your card.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data really private?

Absolutely. save-book-extension stores everything locally in your browser's storage. There are no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Nothing ever leaves your computer. Even we (the developers) cannot see your data.

Will this work on other browsers?

This version is specifically designed for Google Chrome. Other Chromium-based browsers (like Microsoft Edge, Brave, or Opera) may also work, but we officially support Chrome.

What if I forget my information?

Since everything is stored locally, it's a good idea to keep a backup. You can export your data from the extension settings (look for the export option) and save it somewhere safe.

Is this free?

Yes! save-book-extension is completely free to use. It's a passion project built by developers who believe in privacy-first tools.
